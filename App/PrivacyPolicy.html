Privacy Policy

The native farm-management features work without an account and do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your records

Farm names, collection dates, egg counts, label designs, sales amounts, customer names and notes are stored in the app on your device. Collection summaries are shared locally with the widget. These records may be included in system backups, depending on your device settings.

Printing and exports

Label PDFs and CSV reports are generated on the device. When you save or share a file, the destination app, printer or service receives that file. Its own privacy rules apply.

Web content

At launch, the app contacts api.roost-super-brint.site with its application identifier. Push-token, advertising-ID and AppsFlyer-ID fields are empty by default. The server may return a web address to display inside the app. That address and cookies are stored locally for later launches. Information you enter on web pages is sent to the relevant website and is subject to that website's policies.

Deleting data

You can delete saved designs, sales and stock adjustments in the app. Delete linked activity records before deleting a collection to keep the stock ledger consistent. Removing a sale or adjustment restores its quantity to the collection. Removing the app deletes its local storage; shared widget data, exported files and backups may need to be removed separately.

Contact

Use Contact Us in the app or visit https://roost-super-brint.site/contact-us.
