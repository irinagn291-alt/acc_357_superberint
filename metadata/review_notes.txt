No permission prompts appear, and the app declares no permission usage-description string.

There is no login, in-app purchase, account deletion flow or App Tracking Transparency prompt. Users can enter farm names, notes, collection data, customer names and activity.

SuperBerint helps egg keepers record collections, track stock and sales, and plan cartons. It also lets users design egg-carton labels and prepare printable sheets.

On a cold launch, a progress indicator appears, followed by “Overview”. There is no onboarding sequence, and no credentials or demo account are needed. Tap “Log a collection” to open “Collections” on “Planner”. Enter “Eggs collected”, set “Carton capacity” and tap “Save collection”. Open “Stock” and select a collection to see “Collection stock”; tap “Record a sale” or “Household use or waste” to record activity. Open “Sales” to review “Sales & activity”, and open “Insights” to review results or use “Export period as CSV”. Open “Labels” to choose a “Label style”, edit “Label details”, save a design or tap “Print design” to prepare a sheet. “Help” opens instructions under “From collection to carton”.

Designs, collections and activity are stored on the device; users can explicitly share a PDF or export a CSV, and the app also makes a network request during launch. “Contact Us” is available in “Help”.

The native interface uses English and US formatting; currency is selectable independently of region.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork is indicated in the brief; the app records collections, stock and sales and prepares labels.

The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations.
